## Notes on design and limitations ⚠️

- This is a baseline, rule-based extractor (fast to deploy, good for bootstrapping).
- The rule-based approach covers many recurring patterns in the input data and will be limited for ambiguous or free-form texts.
- Relation extraction uses heuristics from sentence context; more complex cases will require a richer parsing or an ML-based model.
- `relations.json` contains placeholders for 30 relations; not all relations are fully derived from the dataset (some remain placeholders).
- The `gold` samples provided are limited for testing: you should extend `kgeb/data/gold_*` to make realistic benchmark sets.
- Currently, some matching logic (e.g., for department, teams, products) is heuristic and conservative; a real evaluation requires improved extraction and a richer gold set.
